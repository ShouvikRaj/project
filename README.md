This study examines subtropical jet stream dynamics
using JRA-55 reanalysis data (1958-2019), focusing
on thermal wind balance and seasonal variations.
The analysis confirms jet cores at 200 hPa near 30◦
latitude, with winter jets exhibiting 50 m s−1 winds
compared to summer’s 35 m s−1
. These findings align
with atmospheric dynamics principles from Stull
(2017), demonstrating how temperature gradients
drive vertical wind shear through the thermal wind
relationship. The study further explores jet stream
positioning relative to Hadley cell boundaries and
implications for atmospheric heat transport.
